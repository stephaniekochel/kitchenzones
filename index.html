Meet The Author

Priya Sankar

Priya Sankar’s first book developed from her life long passion of cooking and baking. Her time spent in the kitchen helped her when remodeling her own families. However it wasn’t easy. Contractors suggested builds that architects advised against. Finding a contractor and architect who work well together and with you is difficult. With her firsthand experience and love for kitchens, she wrote Kitchen Zones accompanied a helpful workbook.

Kitchen Zones

Remodeling advice that thinks about YOU!

You’ve decided it’s time to remodel your kitchen—or you’re seriously thinking about it. The possibilities are exciting, but the process can quickly become overwhelming. Between design decisions, budgets, contractors, and unexpected surprises, many homeowners feel unprepared for what a kitchen remodel truly involves.

This step-by-step guide to worry-free kitchen Remodeling helps you approach the process with clarity and confidence.

Written by an attorney with the perspective of being a homeowner, this practical guide cuts out the sales pitches and gathers advice from unbiased experts to give you a clear roadmap for planning and managing your remodel. You’ll learn how to design a kitchen that works for your family, budget your project wisely, avoid common remodeling mistakes, and hire the right team. The book includes a practical workbook with checklists, worksheets, kitchen layout grids, and budget templates to help plan every step of the way.
